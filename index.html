Rumors

There are several versions of the beginning.

The most common one places And Venus in Austin, Texas, in the early 2000s. The band was formed by a group of friends who shared an interest in experimental music and the occult. They began performing together in small venues around the city, quickly gaining a following for their unique sound and enigmatic presence.

And Venus hails from The Rio Grande Valley, a region known for its rich cultural heritage and vibrant music scene. The band draws inspiration from the diverse sounds and traditions of the area, blending them with their own experimental style to create something truly unique.
