About

About And Venus

And Venus is an alternative rock band hailing from the Rio Grande Valley and Austin, Texas. Formed by a group of longtime friends, the band combines dynamic guitar work, driving rhythm, and emotive melodies to craft an unconventional, captivating sound.

Known for their intense live shows and passionate performances, And Venus draws inspiration from diverse musical traditions, blending atmospheric textures with raw energy.
