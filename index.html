Rumors

There are several versions of the beginning.

The band was formed in the early to mid 2000s by a group of friends who shared an interest in experimental music and the occult. They began performing together in small venues around Austin, Texas, and quickly gained a reputation for their intense live shows and unconventional approach to music. Over the years, they have released an EP, and have played extensively throughout Texas.

And Venus hails from The Rio Grande Valley, a region known for its rich cultural heritage and vibrant music scene. The band draws inspiration from the diverse sounds and traditions of the area, blending them with their own experimental style to create something truly unique.
